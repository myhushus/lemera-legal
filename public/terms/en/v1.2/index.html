4. What Lemera does

Lemera is a portrait-only group camera app. Depending on how you use it, Lemera may:

  • use the device camera to capture photos and to scan a host QR code;
  • create groups using LocalOnlyHotspot on Android or Apple peer-to-peer connections on iPhone;
  • show a Host QR containing local connection invitation information for the relevant platform;
  • join through the Host QR or supported six-digit BLE code; Bluetooth is used for discovery and invitation exchange, not photo transfer;
  • send captured JPEGs and join/control/sharing metadata needed for the current group over local device connections, not through a Meroviq server;
  • save photos to the system photo library; Android 10 and later can group the same saved photo into a group folder after successful sharing or receipt, while iOS saves to the general Photos library without creating a group-named system album;
  • store local session metadata (such as group title, member alias, roster, and share status) in on-device preferences;
  • open these legal pages in the device browser, open the device mail app for Contact Us, or share the public Download install link through the system share sheet.

Android and iPhone Members can join a group hosted on Android. Only iPhone Members can join a group hosted on iPhone; Android Members cannot join an iPhone Host.

5. Your content and local sharing

You keep any rights you have in photos and text you create in Lemera. You are responsible for the photos you capture and for what you share with other people in a local group.

The in-app Album contains the current active group only. Photos are saved to the device’s system photo library. On Android 10 and later, successful sharing or receipt can group the same saved photo under Pictures/Lemera/{group title}/ without a second JPEG copy; your gallery may display this folder using its own presentation. On iOS, Lemera saves to the general Photos library and does not create a group-named system album. A temporary network interruption or closing the app is not End Group. The active group can be restored and eligible missing photos can be shared after reconnecting to the same group, subject to operating-system permissions and network availability. Automatic recovery is not guaranteed.

Explicit End Group stops sharing and recovery on the Host and clears its internal session and pending-sharing records. Leave or Remove clears the affected member’s session when that action is processed. Other devices clear their session when they receive and process the ending or removal signal; immediate cleanup on disconnected devices is not guaranteed. Ended sessions are not restored for later backlog delivery. Already saved system Photos/Gallery assets remain. Closing the app or uninstalling it does not itself remove those saved photos.

Within the current session, withdrawing your own photo stops its sharing and can request removal of known received copies; your own original in the system photo library remains. Removal of a received system photo depends on the operating system and the permissions or approval it requires. A disconnected device, refused deletion, or copies made outside Lemera can prevent or delay removal. This is not a guarantee of erasure from every participant’s device.
