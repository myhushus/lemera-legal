4. What Lemera does

Lemera is a portrait-only group camera app. Depending on how you use it, Lemera may:

  • use the device camera to capture photos and to scan a host QR code;
  • on Android, start a device LocalOnlyHotspot so nearby members can join the same local group;
  • show a host QR code that contains local Wi-Fi credentials and a local join endpoint (SSID, passphrase, host IP address, and join port);
  • let a member join by scanning that QR code and connecting to the host hotspot;
  • send captured JPEG photos, and local join/control/backlog metadata (for example member and group identifiers, sharing state, leave/remove/end metadata, and photoId/backlog requests), to other current group members over TCP on the local hotspot subnet (LAN P2P), not through a Meroviq server;
  • save captured photos, and JPEGs received from other members, to the device Photos/Gallery and, after a successful local share, link that same asset into a group-named album without making a second JPEG copy;
  • store local session metadata (such as group title, member alias, roster, and share status) in on-device preferences;
  • open these legal pages in the device browser, open the device mail app for Contact Us, or share the public Play Store install link through the system share sheet.

5. Your content and local sharing

You keep any rights you have in photos and text you create in Lemera. You are responsible for the photos you capture and for what you share with other people in a local group.

When you share a photo in a group, that JPEG is transmitted on the local network to other current group members’ devices. Recipients save that JPEG to their device Photos/Gallery and, after a successful local share, may link the same asset into the group-named album without making a second JPEG copy. Meroviq does not receive, host, or store those photos on a Meroviq server.

Ending a group stops new sharing. Photos captured before the end time that were eligible but not yet received may still be delivered in a short backlog settlement immediately after End Group. Photos saved to Photos/Gallery remain under the device operating system’s photo library. Uninstalling Lemera does not by itself delete photos already stored in Photos/Gallery.
