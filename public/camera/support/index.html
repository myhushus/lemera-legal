Lemera Camera Support

Help for Lemera Camera 1.0.0 on iPhone and iPad.

Lemera takes photos and saves them to your device's photo library. You do not need an account or sign-in to use the camera.

Take a photo

Allow camera access when prompted, frame your photo, and tap the shutter button. Use the camera switch to change between available front and rear cameras. Pinch the preview to zoom, or tap it to set focus and exposure. Flash options depend on the camera hardware.

Save and check your photos

Photos are saved to your device's photo library. Allow Lemera to add photos when iOS asks. After a successful save, tap the small thumbnail to view the most recently saved photo in the system preview. You can also find saved photos in the Photos app.

If saving fails, allow photo saving in Settings if needed, check that your device has free storage, and tap Retry in Lemera. A photo waiting to be saved must be saved before you can take another one.

Use the shooting tools

Use the top controls to choose a 1:1, 4:3, or 16:9 photo ratio, or a 3, 5, or 10 second timer. Tap the shutter again to cancel a running timer.

Open Camera tools to turn the grid, level, date and time stamp, or memo stamp on or off. A memo can contain up to 40 characters. Date, time, and memo stamps are permanently added to saved photos when enabled. The grid and level are preview guides and do not appear in saved photos.

Use the level

Turn on Level in Camera tools and hold your device upright while composing the photo. Follow the level guide to adjust the tilt. If the device is lying flat, tilt it up. The level depends on the device's motion sensors; it may be unavailable when sensor data cannot be read.

Troubleshoot permissions

  • Camera: If camera access is denied, tap Open Settings in the app's permission message and allow camera access for Lemera. Return to Lemera and tap Retry if the message remains.
  • Photos: Allow Lemera to add photos to your photo library. The app saves new photos without requesting access to browse your full photo library.
  • Level: If iOS asks for motion access, allow it to use the level. Check the device's privacy settings if access was denied.

If the preview does not start, return to the app and try again. Permission names and the layout of Settings may vary by iOS version.

Contact us

Email lemera@meroviq.com for help. Include your device model, iOS version, Lemera version, and the steps that led to the problem. Please avoid sending private photos or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to explain the issue.
