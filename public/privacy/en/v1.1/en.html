Lemera Privacy Policy

Version: 1.1
Effective date: October 1, 2026
Privacy contact: lemera@meroviq.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Meroviq (“Meroviq,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) processes information in connection with the Lemera mobile application (“Lemera”). The current version of Lemera is a camera app that captures and saves photos on your device. Lemera does not create a Meroviq account and does not upload captured photos to a Meroviq server or cloud backend.

1. Who we are

Lemera is provided by Meroviq. For privacy questions, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

2. Information processed in Lemera

3. Information we do not collect

The current Lemera app does not include a Meroviq user account or sign-in, Firebase or another Meroviq cloud backend, advertising or advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, a remote crash-reporting service, in-app purchases or payment processing, user-to-user photo or content sharing, location collection or tracking, contact collection, microphone recording, or advertising identifier use.

Lemera does not collect photos you capture on a server operated by Meroviq.

4. Device permissions

Lemera requests only the permissions needed to provide its camera functions:

  • Camera — used to display the camera preview and capture photos.
  • Storage write access on Android 9 / API 28 and earlier — may be used on older Android devices to save captured photos to device photo storage.

The current Android version does not request broad photo or media read access to browse your existing photo library. It does not request microphone, location, or advertising ID permission. If you deny camera permission, Lemera cannot capture photos.

5. How information is used

  • to display the camera preview;
  • to capture photos you request;
  • to save captured photos to your device's Photos or Gallery storage; and
  • to respond if you contact us by email.

6. Sharing

Meroviq does not collect, sell, or upload photos captured with Lemera. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

The current Lemera app does not transmit captured photos or app usage data to Meroviq servers, advertising partners, or analytics services.

If you email us, we may use the information to respond to your inquiry and may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect rights and safety.

7. International processing

The current Lemera app does not transfer captured photos or app usage data to a Meroviq cloud server. If you contact us by email, the email service provider involved may process the message in other countries according to its own privacy practices.

8. Retention

Captured photos are stored in your device's Photos or Gallery storage and may remain there until you delete them. Meroviq does not retain a server copy of those photos.

If you email us, we may retain the correspondence as needed to respond to your request, maintain support records, protect our rights, and comply with applicable law.

9. Your choices

You can change camera permission in device settings, delete captured photos through your device's Photos or Gallery app, and uninstall Lemera.

Because the current Lemera app does not have a Meroviq user account or cloud photo storage, there is no cloud account deletion process for the app. To exercise applicable privacy rights regarding information Meroviq actually holds, such as an email you sent us, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

10. Security

The current Lemera app does not transmit captured photos to a Meroviq server. Photos are stored on your device and are protected by the operating system and your device security settings. No method of device storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children

Lemera is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Lemera changes or as required by applicable law. An updated version will be posted on this site with a new version number and effective date.
