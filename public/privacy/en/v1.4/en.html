Lemera Privacy Policy

1.4
Draft — not yet effective. The effective date will be set at publication approval.

This Privacy Policy explains how Meroviq (“Meroviq,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) processes information in connection with the Lemera mobile application (“Lemera”). The current Lemera app is a local group camera that lets nearby participants join a temporary group and share photos directly between their devices. Lemera does not require a Meroviq account and does not upload group photos to a Meroviq cloud backend.

1. Who we are

Lemera is provided by Meroviq. For privacy questions, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

3. Local sharing, blocking, and reporting

Lemera is designed for temporary, invite-based local groups rather than a public social feed. Photos are shared only among participants in the current group over the local device connection.

Blocking a participant stops new photo sharing between the two participants in both directions. Existing photos already shared remain on the relevant devices. Photos captured during a block are not sent later merely because the block is removed. Either participant may later unblock sharing where the app provides that control.

Members can block or report other members in the current group. Reports are sent locally to the current Host, who can keep the member or remove the member from the group. The Host does not report members, and members are not offered controls to block or report the Host. Members can leave the group. Reports are limited to the active session and are cleared when the group ends; no report is sent to an external moderation server.

Android and iPhone Members can join a group hosted on Android. Only iPhone Members can join a group hosted on iPhone; Android Members cannot join an iPhone Host.

4. Information we do not collect

The current Lemera app does not require a Meroviq user account or sign-in and does not use a Meroviq cloud backend for photo storage, group history, reports, or identity. It does not integrate advertising SDKs or a separate Meroviq analytics service and does not intentionally collect contacts, microphone recordings, health information, government identifiers, payment card numbers, advertising identifiers, or precise geographic coordinates.

Android 1.1.0 (versionCode 13) uses ZXing-C++ to process QR images and recognition results on-device. This version does not include Google ML Kit or its diagnostic-transmission dependencies. iOS continues to use Apple Vision / AVFoundation.

In earlier Android versions containing Google ML Kit, QR images and recognition results are processed on-device, but Google documents transmission of device/app information, installation identifiers, and technical performance/usage information to Google for SDK diagnostics, maintenance and improvement. This is separate from local photo sharing. This explanation continues to apply while using those versions; updating does not mean that information already received by Google is deleted. Lemera does not maintain past-group photo indexes or historical group records in a Meroviq cloud service. Photos already saved to a participant's device may remain there under that participant's control after the group ends.

5. Device permissions

Lemera requests operating-system permissions only as needed for the features you use:

  • Camera — to capture photos and scan a Host QR code.
  • Photos / Gallery — to save captured or received photos as supported by the operating system. Older Android versions may require legacy storage permission for saving.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi Devices (supported newer Android versions) — to create or use the local group connection. This permission is not used by Lemera to determine your geographic location.
  • Location (certain older Android versions) — Android may require location permission to create or manage a local Wi-Fi hotspot. Lemera uses this permission only to satisfy the operating-system requirement for the local connection and is not designed to determine, collect, store, or share your precise geographic location.
  • Wi-Fi / network state and Internet/network access — to create or join the local connection and exchange join, control, report, and photo-sharing traffic between current group devices. Lemera does not use this permission to upload group photos or reports to a Meroviq cloud backend.
  • Bluetooth — nearby group discovery and invitation exchange for code joining and supported reconnection; never photo transfer.
  • Notifications / connected-device foreground service (Android) — show ongoing Host sharing while supporting local connections in the background. No background camera capture.
  • iOS Photos — request add-only access when saving; request read/write access only when a necessary system-photo deletion cannot use existing permission. For a custom profile photo, you can choose an image through the system picker; Lemera imports that chosen image for cropping and a locally saved profile thumbnail. It does not scan your entire photo library.
  • Local Network (iOS) — nearby group discovery and connections, including Bonjour and supported Apple peer-to-peer hosting.

You can change permissions in device settings. Denying a required permission may prevent the related feature from working.

6. How information is used

  • to create and join the current local group;
  • to display the current roster and participant profile information;
  • to capture, save, send, and receive photos within the current group;
  • to enforce Block/Unblock sharing state;
  • to deliver session-scoped reports to the current Host and allow group-level moderation;
  • to end or leave the current group and clear temporary session state; and
  • to respond if you email us.

7. Sharing and disclosure

Meroviq does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

When you participate in a Lemera group, information needed for the group experience may be shared directly with other current participants, including your display information, photos you share, local participant/session identifiers, and control information necessary to operate the group. This is device-to-device sharing initiated by participation in the local group, not a Meroviq cloud upload.

Session-scoped reports are shared locally with the current Host as described above. They are not sent to a Meroviq moderation server.

If you email us, we may use the information to respond to your inquiry and may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect rights and safety.

8. Precise location

Lemera does not share your current precise physical location with other users. On certain older Android versions, location permission may be required by Android for LocalOnlyHotspot or related local Wi-Fi functionality. Lemera does not use that permission to create a location history, determine your precise coordinates for app features, or transmit precise location to group participants or Meroviq.

9. International processing

Meroviq does not operate a Lemera cloud database that receives group photos, local group metadata, or session reports. Local group sharing remains between participating devices on the local connection. If you email us, the email service provider involved may process the message in other countries according to its own privacy practices.

10. Retention

Active-session records are retained on the device while needed for the current group, including restoration after an app restart. End Group, Leave, Remove, or receipt of a Host-end signal clears that device’s session records and pending-sharing state. A disconnected device cannot process an ending signal until it receives it; immediate deletion on every offline peer is not guaranteed. Ended sessions are not restored as past groups.

Reports are held in memory for the current group and are cleared when the group ends.

Photos saved to Photos/Gallery can remain on the relevant participant's device until that participant deletes them. Meroviq does not keep a server copy of those group photos.

If you email us, we may retain the correspondence as needed to respond to your request, maintain support records, protect our rights, and comply with applicable law.

Your own locally saved profile is separate from session records and survives group ending and app restarts so it can be reused. Ending a group does not erase that profile. Saved system photos also remain under the device owner’s control. Copies already received by other participants cannot be remotely recalled by Meroviq.

Lemera does not operate its own cloud photo backup. System Photos synchronization, operating-system backups, device migration and copies you make are separate services under your device settings and their providers’ terms. Lemera does not promise that every local profile or preference is excluded from those backups; a backup or migration can restore such data after reinstallation.

11. Your choices

You can control participation by joining or leaving a group, use Block/Unblock to control new photo sharing with another participant, and delete saved photos through your device's Photos/Gallery app.

The Host can keep or remove a reported member. Members can block or unblock sharing with other members, or leave the group. Blocking and reporting the Host are not offered in the current interface.

Because Lemera does not have a Meroviq user account or cloud photo storage, there is no Meroviq cloud account deletion process for the current app. To exercise applicable privacy rights regarding information Meroviq actually holds, such as an email you sent us, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

Within the current session, withdrawing your own photo stops its sharing and can request removal of known received copies; your own original in the system photo library remains. Removal of a received system photo depends on the operating system and the permissions or approval it requires. A disconnected device, refused deletion, or copies made outside Lemera can prevent or delay removal. This is not a guarantee of erasure from every participant’s device.

12. Security

Groups hosted on iPhone use authenticated, encrypted Apple peer-to-peer connections. Groups hosted on Android, including those joined by iPhone Members, use local TCP and Wi-Fi connection security without a separate application-layer TLS channel. Lemera does not promise a separate end-to-end encryption layer covering every group connection. Limit group and local-network access to people you trust. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

13. Children

Lemera is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

14. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Lemera changes or as required by applicable law. An updated version will be posted on this site with a new version number and effective date.
