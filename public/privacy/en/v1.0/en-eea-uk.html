Lemera Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective date: August 17, 2026
Privacy contact: lemera@meroviq.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Meroviq (“Meroviq,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) processes information in connection with the Lemera mobile application (“Lemera”). Lemera is a local group camera app. It does not create a Meroviq account and does not upload photos or group data to a Meroviq cloud backend.

1. Who we are

Lemera is provided by Meroviq. For privacy questions, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

2. Information processed in Lemera

Depending on how you use Lemera, the following may be processed. Meroviq does not collect this information on a Meroviq server unless you email us.

3. Information we do not collect

The current Lemera app does not include a Meroviq user account, Firebase or other cloud backend, advertising, analytics SDK, crash-reporting backend, or in-app purchase / premium entitlement processing. Lemera does not intentionally collect contacts, microphone recordings, health data, government identifiers, or payment card numbers.

On Android 12 and earlier, the operating system may require location permission to start LocalOnlyHotspot. Lemera uses that OS requirement to start a local hotspot. The app is not designed to collect or store your geographic location, and the Nearby Wi-Fi Devices permission is declared as never used for location.

4. Device permissions

Lemera asks the operating system for permissions only as needed for the feature you use:

  • Camera — to capture photos and to scan a host QR code.
  • Photos / Gallery — to save captured JPEGs and JPEGs received from other members to the general library and, after a successful local share, to link that same asset into a group-named album without a second copy. On older Android versions, a legacy storage permission may be required to insert into MediaStore.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi Devices (Android 13+) — to create or join the local group hotspot. Declared as never for location.
  • Location (fine) (Android 12 and earlier, limited SDK range) — required by Android to start LocalOnlyHotspot; not used to build a location history.
  • Wi-Fi / network state — to start or join the local hotspot and to exchange join, photo, and related control/backlog traffic on that subnet.
  • Internet — declared for local LAN TCP photo share, join registration, and related control/backlog traffic on the hotspot subnet, not for uploading photos to a Meroviq server. Opening legal pages or Contact Us uses the device browser or mail app.

You can change permissions in device settings. If you deny camera permission, Lemera cannot capture photos or scan QR codes.

5. How information is used

  • to run the local group camera, hotspot, QR join, and LAN photo-share features you request;
  • to save photos you capture or receive to Photos/Gallery and link successfully shared photos into a group album on the same device;
  • to remember alias, group title, roster, and share metadata on the device across app launches;
  • to open these legal pages, Contact Us email, or the Play Store install link when you choose those actions;
  • to respond if you email lemera@meroviq.com.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not send Lemera photos or group metadata to Firebase, Google Cloud app backends, or advertising partners.

If you join a local group, other current members receive the information needed to participate (for example alias, photos you share, and join/control/backlog metadata) over the local network. That sharing is between devices you choose to connect, not a Meroviq cloud transfer.

If you email us, we use the message to respond to your inquiry. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

7. International processing

Meroviq does not operate a Lemera cloud database that transfers your photos or group metadata internationally. Local LAN sharing stays on the local network. If you email us, your email provider may process that message according to its own terms.

8. Retention

On-device preferences remain until you clear app data or uninstall Lemera. Photos saved to Photos/Gallery remain until you delete them in the device photo app or another gallery tool. Photos already received by other group members remain on those members’ devices under their control.

If you email us, we keep the correspondence as needed to handle the request and as required by law.

9. Your choices

You can stop new local sharing by leaving or ending the group, turning off sharing for a member where the app provides that control, or uninstalling Lemera. After End Group, new sharing stops, but photos captured before the end time that were eligible and not yet received may still be delivered in a short backlog settlement immediately after the group ends. You can delete photos in Photos/Gallery. Because Lemera has no Meroviq account, there is no cloud account deletion flow in the current app.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information we hold (for example email you sent us). Contact lemera@meroviq.com.

10. Security

Local photo share uses TCP on the hotspot subnet without an additional application-layer encryption protocol in the current implementation. Anyone who can join the local hotspot and group can receive shared photos. Use Lemera only with people you trust on that local network. No method of local transmission or device storage is completely secure.

11. Children

Lemera is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact lemera@meroviq.com.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The updated version will be posted on this site with a new effective date. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice where required.
